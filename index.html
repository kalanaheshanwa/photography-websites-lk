BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Everything you may want to ask.

Clear expectations make a better working relationship. If your question is not here, contact us directly.

Ask on WhatsApp ↗
01Why is this a monthly service?+

Your monthly payment covers the website design, hosting, maintenance, technical care and ongoing content updates. You avoid a large development payment and never need to manage the website yourself.

02Are there any setup costs or hidden charges?+

No. Your selected monthly price covers the website design, standard domain, hosting, maintenance and the included updates. You can start from LKR 5,000 with one clear monthly fee.

03Can I cancel the subscription anytime?+

Yes. There is no six-month commitment. You can cancel future renewals at any time. Your website remains active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

04Will my website look like every other photographer’s website?+

No. We reuse a reliable technical system, but your typography, colours, page composition and gallery treatment are designed around your own photography and brand.

05How do I send new portfolio images?+

Simply send the selected images and instructions through WhatsApp. We optimise them for fast loading and add them to the correct gallery.

06Can I change my photography packages and prices?+

Yes. Text, price, package and portfolio changes are included within the monthly update allowance of your selected plan.

07What counts as one monthly update?+

One update can include a small group of related changes, such as replacing up to 15 gallery images, updating one package, or changing existing text and contact details.

08What happens if I need more updates?+

We will confirm the additional cost before starting. You can also move to a higher plan if you regularly need more changes.

09Will you manage my website for as long as I subscribe?+

Yes. While your subscription remains active, we guarantee ongoing hosting, technical care and content-update support within your plan. There is no expiry after the first year.

10What happens if a monthly payment fails?+

We keep the website active for a 14-day grace period and contact you so the payment can be corrected. If payment is still outstanding after 14 days, the website may be temporarily paused. It can be restored after the account is brought up to date.

11How will the domain be purchased and managed?+

You can choose either option. We can purchase and manage the standard domain for you, or you can purchase it yourself and give us the required access. Your monthly website price remains exactly the same with either option.

12Will this be my own newly designed website?+

Yes. You receive a new website designed around your photography, services, colours and brand. Your photographs, logo and written business content remain yours.

13Can customers book directly through the website?+

The standard journey sends potential clients to your WhatsApp with a clear enquiry prompt. A full booking or payment system can be added as a separately priced upgrade.

14How quickly can my website be launched?+

Most websites can be launched within 5–7 working days after all required photographs, packages, branding and contact details are received.

15What happens after I cancel?+

Future monthly renewals stop. The managed website remains active until the end of the period already paid for, and your original photographs and business content remain yours.
